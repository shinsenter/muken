# The name "muken"

The Apache License 2.0 covers the code. It does not give you the right to use the name of the project (section 6 of the license). This page says what you can and cannot do with the name.

"muken" is the name of this project, and Mai Nhut Tan reserves all rights to it. The name is not registered as a trademark at this time.

## You can

- Say that your product works with muken, uses muken, or is based on muken. Be accurate about it.
- Write about muken, teach it, and link to this repository.
- Call a tool you build "a deploy tool for muken" or "for use with muken".
- Keep the name in your copy of the code and in your own notes, as the license requires you to keep the notices.

## Please do not

- Name your fork, product, company or service "muken", or something that can be mistaken for it (for example "muken Pro", "mukenhq"), unless the maintainer has agreed in writing.
- Suggest that your version is the official one, or that the maintainer endorses it.
- Use the project's logo or look, if one is added later, in a way that suggests the same.

If you publish a modified version, give it a different name and say that it is based on muken.

Open an issue in this repository, or write to the maintainer, Mai Nhut Tan <shin@shin.company>. Asking is quick and almost always ends with a yes.
